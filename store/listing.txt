NAME (max 75)
PUPSync

SHORT DESCRIPTION (max 132 characters)
Import your PUP SIAS class schedule into Google Calendar and check GWA / Latin honors standing.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION
PUPSync helps PUP students move from SIAS into a calendar they actually use — and see academic standing at a glance.

Schedule → Google Calendar
• Open your SIAS schedule page, then open PUPSync
• Review a week grid or subject list, pick colors, preview events
• Import recurring class meetings to your primary Google Calendar

Grades → GWA & standing
• Open your SIAS grades page, then open PUPSync
• See overall GWA (NSTP excluded) and indicative Latin honors standing

Privacy
• Reads schedule/grades/name only from pages already open in your browser
• No PUPSync server — your data stays on your device except when you authorize Google Calendar
• Full policy: [PASTE PRIVACY POLICY URL HERE]

Not affiliated with the Polytechnic University of the Philippines.

SINGLE PURPOSE
Help PUP students sync SIAS class schedules to Google Calendar and view GWA / honors standing from SIAS grades.

PERMISSION JUSTIFICATIONS (for store questionnaire)
• identity — Sign in with Google to create Calendar events when importing a schedule
• storage — Save color choices, semester dates, greeting name, and temporary auth token locally
• scripting + activeTab — Read the SIAS schedule/grades/home DOM on the active tab when you open the popup
• host *.pup.edu.ph — Access SIAS pages to parse schedule, grades, and student name
• host www.googleapis.com — Call Google Calendar API to create events
• OAuth scope calendar.events — Create (not delete/list-all) events on the user’s primary calendar
