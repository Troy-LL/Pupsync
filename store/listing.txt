NAME (max 75)
PUPSync

SUMMARY (max 132 characters; the store takes this from the manifest description, which matches)
Import your PUPSIS (PUP SIS) class schedule into Google Calendar in a few clicks. See your GWA and Latin honors standing too.

DESCRIPTION
Get your PUPSIS class schedule into Google Calendar without typing every class by hand.

Open your schedule on PUP SIS, open PUPSync, check the preview, then import. Classes repeat every week, no-class days are skipped, and you pick the colors.

There's no PUPSync server. Your schedule goes from your browser straight to your own Google Calendar, through access Google reviewed and approved.

Your PUPSIS schedule, in Google Calendar
• Open the Schedule page on SIAS and PUPSync reads your classes
• See your week as a grid or a list before anything is added
• Pick a color for each subject, rename classes, and edit the event description
• Holidays, breaks and exam days for the term are skipped for you
• Changed sections or dropped a class? Import again. PUPSync updates your classes and removes dropped ones instead of making duplicates
• Remove PUPSync classes takes this sem's classes off your calendar. Events you made yourself are never touched
• Export your week as an image to share with your block

New sem? Open your new schedule and PUPSync offers to import it. Last sem's classes stay as they are.

GWA and honors, as a bonus
• Open the Grades page on SIAS to see your overall GWA (NSTP not included)
• See your estimated Latin honors and President's or Dean's Lister standing. It's indicative only; your registrar has the final say

Your data
• PUPSync only reads SIAS pages you open, on *.pup.edu.ph
• It only writes the class events you choose to import, to your own Google Calendar
• Your settings stay in your browser. The Google sign-in token is kept only until you close the browser
• The code is public on GitHub: https://github.com/Troy-LL/Pupsync
• Privacy policy: https://pupsync.niched.tech/privacy-policy.html

Questions or something broke? Use Report a problem in the popup, or email support@niched.tech.

SINGLE PURPOSE
Import a PUP student's class schedule from the SIAS schedule page into their Google Calendar, with a GWA view from the SIAS grades page.

PERMISSION JUSTIFICATIONS
• identity: Sign in with Google when you import, so PUPSync can add your classes to your Google Calendar.
• storage: Save your colors, class names, description template, semester dates, greeting name and the IDs of the class events PUPSync made, on your device. The sign-in token is kept in session storage and cleared when the browser closes.
• scripting and activeTab: Read the schedule, grades or home page on the SIAS tab you have open when you use the popup.
• Host *.pup.edu.ph: Read SIAS pages (schedule, grades, home) and know when a SIAS tab is open so the popup can open once.
• Host www.googleapis.com: Call the Google Calendar API.
• OAuth scope calendar.events: Adds your classes to your primary Google Calendar and updates or removes only the events PUPSync made (marked with a private PUPSync tag), for example when you import again, drop a class, or click Remove PUPSync classes.
• tabs: no longer requested. Removed in this version.

DATA USAGE DISCLOSURE (store form)
Collected and handled on the device only: personally identifiable info (name shown on SIAS, for the greeting), website content (schedule and grades), authentication info (Google token, session only). Not sold, not used for anything unrelated to the single purpose, not used for credit or lending.
