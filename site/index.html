How it works

Three moves. No backend. Your SIAS tab is the source of truth.

  1. Open SIAS Schedule or grades: the extension reads the page you’re already on.
  2. Click PUPSync Preview the week grid, pick colors, or check GWA and indicative Latin standing.
  3. Import or share Send classes to Google Calendar, export an image, or keep the standing glance to yourself.

What it actually does

Capabilities, not vibes: the jobs the extension already ships.

  • Schedule → Google Calendar

    Parse SIAS class meetings, color them, preview the week, then import with your Google account.

  • GWA & Latin standing

    Read overall GWA from the grades page and see indicative Cum / Magna / Summa cutoffs. Registrar still wins.

  • Landing hub on SIAS

    From any SIAS page, get a first-name greeting and a clear route to schedule or grades.

  • Share the clean cut

    Export a week image or show standing without pasting your whole academic life into the group chat.

Why I made it

Honestly? I don’t like making my own schedules. Most of the time I just don’t. I raw-dog the semester and hope the rooms are where I left them in my head.

I also kept opening grades like a nervous tick: am I still in Latin or not? I wanted a calmer way to check, and a way to share the useful bit with friends without dumping everything.

PUPSync is my attempt at a fun, low-friction loop: sync the week, glance the standing, stay a little more motivated about the work I’m already doing.

~ a PUP student who got tired of the spreadsheet theater
