How it works

Three moves. No backend. Your SIAS tab is the source of truth.

  1. Open SIAS Schedule or grades: the extension reads the page you’re already on.
  2. Click PUPSync Preview the week grid, pick colors, or check GWA standing.
  3. Import or share Send classes to Google Calendar, export an image, or keep the glance private.

Why I made it

Honestly? I don’t like making my own schedules. Most of the time I just don’t. I raw-dog the semester and hope the rooms are where I left them in my head.

I also kept opening grades like a nervous tick: am I still in Latin or not? I wanted a calmer way to check, and a way to share the useful bit with friends without dumping everything.

~ a PUP student who got tired of the spreadsheet theater
