    See your week first. Rename or recolor any class.

    Sign in with Google. Classes repeat weekly until the sem ends.

Your data

  • No PUPSync server. It all runs in your browser.
  • Reads only the SIAS pages you open.
  • Changes only its own class events. Yours are never touched.
  • The code is public on GitHub.
Permissions
Google OAuth scope
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.events
Adds your classes as recurring events to your primary Google Calendar. Updates or removes only events PUPSync made (each has a private PUPSync tag). Asked only when you click Import. Revoke anytime in Google Account permissions.
Sign-in token
Kept in Chrome session storage and cleared when you close the browser.
Host *.pup.edu.ph
Reads the SIAS schedule, grades and home pages you open. Notices when a SIAS tab opens, for the "Open on SIAS pages" setting.
Host www.googleapis.com
Talk to the Google Calendar API over HTTPS.
identity, storage, scripting, activeTab
Google sign-in, saving your colors, names and settings on this device, and reading the SIAS tab you're on when you use the popup.

More in the privacy policy.

Questions

Is this official?

No. It's a student project, not made or endorsed by PUP.

Does it work for my campus?

If your schedule is on SIAS at pup.edu.ph, it works. Classes look wrong? Click Report a problem in the popup.

What about next sem?

Import your new SIAS schedule. Last sem's classes stay.

How do I remove the events?

Click Remove PUPSync classes in the popup. Only this sem's PUPSync classes go.

Where do I report a problem?

Use Report a problem in the popup, or email support@niched.tech.
