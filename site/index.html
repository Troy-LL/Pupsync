How it works

  1. Open your SIAS schedule

    Log in to SIAS as usual and open the Schedule page. PUPSync reads the classes on it.

  2. Check and color

    See the week before anything is added. Rename a class or give it a color.

  3. Import

    Sign in with Google once. Every class repeats weekly until the end of the sem.

What it handles that typing by hand doesn't

Holidays and exam days skipped

PUPSync knows the term dates and leaves breaks, holidays and exam weeks empty.

Import again, no duplicates

Changed sections or dropped a class? Import again. Your classes get updated, not copied.

Your colors and class names

Short names like HCI or Netw Adm, and a color per subject, carried into Google Calendar.

Export the week as an image

Save your week as a picture to send to your block.

Your GWA, while you're at it

Open the Grades page on SIAS and PUPSync shows your overall GWA and whether you're on track for Latin honors.

It's an estimate. Your registrar has the final say. NSTP is not counted.

Your data

  • No PUPSync server. Everything runs in your browser. Your schedule goes straight to your own Google Calendar.
  • It reads only the SIAS pages you open, on pup.edu.ph.
  • It writes only the class events you confirm. It updates or removes only events it made, and never touches events you made yourself.
  • The code is public on GitHub.

Questions

Is this official?

No. PUPSync is a student project. It is not made, endorsed or checked by the Polytechnic University of the Philippines.

Does it work for my campus?

It works wherever your schedule is on the SIAS Schedule page at pup.edu.ph. If your classes look wrong in the preview, click Report a problem in the popup so it can be fixed for your campus.

What about next sem?

Open your new schedule on SIAS and PUPSync offers to import it. Last sem's classes stay on your calendar as they are.

How do I remove the events?

Open PUPSync and click Remove PUPSync classes. It takes this sem's PUPSync classes off your calendar and leaves everything else alone.

Where do I report a problem?

Click Report a problem at the bottom of the popup, or email support@niched.tech.
