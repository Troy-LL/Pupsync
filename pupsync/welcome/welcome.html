PUPSync

Get your SIAS schedule into Google Calendar.

Do this once each sem.

  1. Open your SIAS schedule

    Go to the Schedule page and click the PUPSync icon in your toolbar.

  2. Check your classes

    Rename, recolor or untick any class.

  3. Import

    Click Import to Calendar and sign in with Google. Holidays and exam days are skipped.

Open my SIAS schedule

No PUPSync server. Your schedule goes from your browser straight to your Google Calendar. Privacy policy

Changed your mind? Click Remove PUPSync classes in the popup. Your own events stay.
