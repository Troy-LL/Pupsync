PUPSync

You're set. Let's get your PUPSIS schedule into Google Calendar.

You only need to do this once a sem.

  1. Open your schedule on PUP SIS

    Sign in to SIAS, go to the Schedule page, and click the PUPSync icon in your toolbar.

  2. Check and color your classes

    See your week as a grid. Pick colors, rename classes, and untick anything you don't want.

  3. Import

    Click Import to Calendar and sign in with Google. Classes repeat every week, and holidays and exam days are skipped.

Open my SIAS schedule

Your data stays yours. There's no PUPSync server. Your schedule goes from your browser straight to your own Google Calendar. Privacy policy

Changed your mind? The popup has a Remove PUPSync classes button that takes this sem's classes off your calendar. Your own events are never touched.
